FastNote

What the subscription is for

Writing new pages needs a subscription. Monthly and yearly subscriptions are offered, and new subscribers start with a free trial; the prices and trial length are shown in the app before you subscribe.

Your writing, in standard formats

You can export your pages in standard, open formats that you can open without FastNote or anything else from ActioCognita. Backups are for restoring into FastNote and use its own format.

If your subscription ends

You can always open, read, search and export everything you have written, and back it up. Until you subscribe again, new writing pauses, and Spotlight and Siri stop using your page headings and text; Spotlight can still find your pages by date and meeting.
