Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

This policy covers every app published by ActioCognita LLC (“we”, “us”). The first part applies to all of our apps. Each app then has its own section saying exactly what that app does with your information.

Who we are

Our apps are made by ActioCognita LLC. You can reach us at contact@actiocognita.com.

What each app collects

Each app’s section below says what information that app handles, where it is kept, and whether anything reaches us. If a section does not say that something reaches us, it does not.

Information you choose to send us

If you email us, or choose to send us a diagnostics report, a backup or an export from one of our apps, we use it only to answer you and to investigate and fix the problem you reported. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else, and we delete it when we no longer need it for that purpose.

Purchases and crash reports

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details. If you allow it in your device’s settings, Apple may share crash reports with us; they describe what went wrong in the app, not your content.

Your choices

You can ask us what information of yours we hold, if any, and ask us to delete it, by writing to contact@actiocognita.com.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If what an app does with your information changes, we update that app’s section before the version that makes the change is released, and change the date at the top of this page. Significant changes are also described in that app’s release notes.

FastNote

The short version: your notebook stays with you. We do not collect, see or sell it.

Your notebook

Your pages, handwriting, headings and settings are stored on your device. We have no servers and no account system, and the app does not send your notebook to us.

iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, your notebook is stored in the private iCloud database of your own Apple account, so it can appear on your other devices. Apple holds that data under its own privacy policy; we cannot read it. Sync is off until you turn it on, and turning it off never deletes anything.

Handwriting recognition

Reading your handwriting, for search, headings and reminders, happens on your device, using Apple’s on-device recognition and language model. Your writing is not sent anywhere to be read.

Calendar and Reminders

If you allow it, the app reads your calendar on your device to match a page to the meeting you are in, and can add a link to that page to the meeting. If you circle writing to make a reminder, the app creates it in Apple Reminders with a link back to the page. It does not read or change your other reminders. You can withdraw either permission in Settings at any time.

Search, Spotlight and Siri

Page titles are added to your device’s Spotlight index so you can find them, and Siri and Shortcuts actions run on your device.

Backups and exports

Backups and PDF exports are saved to the app’s folder on your device, visible in the Files app. They leave your device only if you move or share them yourself.

Diagnostics

The app keeps a short technical log on your device, about a day’s worth, to help fix problems. It leaves your device only if you choose Share diagnostics and send it to us. It describes how the app is running: the app and system version, the device model, free storage, dates and timings, error messages, and counts and identifiers of pages. It does not contain your handwriting.

What FastNote does not do

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and no third-party software that collects data.
